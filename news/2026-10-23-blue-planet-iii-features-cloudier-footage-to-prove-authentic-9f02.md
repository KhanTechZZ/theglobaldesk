BBC series deliberately uses ‘more organic’ shots to assure viewers animals are not AI-generated, producers say

The [David Attenborough](https://www.theguardian.com/tv-and-radio/david-attenborough)\-narrated series Blue Planet III deliberately includes cloudier, less polished footage to counter AI fakery and assure viewers it is “authentic”, its producers have said.

Nearly a decade after Blue Planet II shone a light on plastic pollution, and 25 years since the first Blue Planet aired, the hit BBC series is returning with more animal behaviour and cutting-edge technology, including the world’s first underwater CCTV camera – dubbed “stonefish” cam.
