The Senate candidate denounced opponent Ken Paxton, calling out corruption allegations and support from Musk

James Talarico, the Democratic candidate for the US Senate in Texas, has taken aim at Elon Musk, warning that the tech mogul is trying to buy political influence and declaring: “Texas is not for sale.”

Talarico [held a campaign rally](https://www.c-span.org/program/campaign-2026/us-senate-candidate-james-talarico-d-tx-makes-a-campaign-tour-stop-in-austin-tx/686930) on Friday at the University of Texas at Austin, packing an auditorium at his alma mater with hundreds of students and other supporters, who cheered, whooped and waved campaign signs.
