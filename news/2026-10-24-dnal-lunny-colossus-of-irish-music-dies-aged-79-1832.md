Founding member of Planxty, Lunny went on to play for bands such as Moving Hearts, the Bothy Band and Coolfin

The Irish traditional musician Dónal Lunny has died at the age of 79 from cancer.

Lunny rose to prominence in the trio Rakes of Kildare alongside his brother Frank and Christy Moore. He went on to play in bands such as [Planxty](https://www.theguardian.com/music/2005/jan/31/popandrock2), [Moving Hearts](https://www.theguardian.com/theguardian/1999/mar/18/features11.g22), the Bothy Band and Coolfin among others and toured the world.
