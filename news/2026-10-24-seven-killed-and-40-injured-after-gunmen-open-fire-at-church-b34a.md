Footage broadcast on state television showed two gunmen who appear to be holding pistols approaching the church and firing shots

Gunmen opened fire during a funeral at a church in Jordan on Friday, killing seven people and wounding 40 others, authorities said.

The shooting took place at a Greek Orthodox church in Karak, about 180km (110 miles) south of the capital, Amman.
