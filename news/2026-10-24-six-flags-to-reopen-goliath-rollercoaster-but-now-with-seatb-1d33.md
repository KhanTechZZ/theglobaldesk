Six Flags will install seatbelts on its Goliath rollercoaster in [Georgia](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/state-of-georgia), after a [rider](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2026/oct/13/six-flags-rollercoaster-death) plummeted to his death earlier this month.

Hassan Jihad Ibn Kariem, a 42-year-old father and educator, was thrown from his seat on the Goliath, a coaster that plunges riders down 165ft, before a second drop of 180ft. On his way down, he collided with the ride’s other cars.
