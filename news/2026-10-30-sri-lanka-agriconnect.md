Sri Lanka, the World Bank Group and the International Fund for Agricultural Development have launched a joint plan to strengthen the country's agrifood sector, create jobs and improve food security.

The plan aims to help 900,000 farmers gain better access to agricultural value chains by 2030. The World Bank says it will be backed by $550 million in its investments and $115 million from IFAD, with the plan also seeking to mobilize private investment.

Its priorities include agricultural finance, digital tools, climate-resilient irrigation, skills and stronger connections between producers and markets. Coconut, cinnamon, tea, fisheries and other value chains are among the areas named in the plan.
