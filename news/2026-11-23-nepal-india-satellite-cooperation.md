Nepalese Prime Minister Balendra Shah made a state visit to India on November 22–23, his first since taking office, according to a joint statement issued after talks with Indian Prime Minister Narendra Modi.

The two sides exchanged eight memoranda of understanding. One covers a satellite communications network using the South Asia Satellite, with Nepal's Ministry of Information and Communication and India's space agency ISRO as partners.

The statement also says India will provide support for post-flood reconstruction and infrastructure in Nepal, alongside a credit line for energy projects. The visit covered broader development and regional cooperation as well.
