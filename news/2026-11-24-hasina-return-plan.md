Former Bangladesh prime minister Sheikh Hasina says she plans to return from India on December 16, the country's Victory Day. She made the announcement while in exile after leaving Bangladesh in August 2024.

Bangladesh's government says it continues to seek her extradition. Hasina has rejected the death sentence handed down against her in absentia and says she is prepared to contest the case if she returns, the Associated Press reported.

Her proposed return date has added to political tension as the country approaches Victory Day. The announcement is a stated plan; whether she can travel back remains uncertain amid the extradition dispute.
