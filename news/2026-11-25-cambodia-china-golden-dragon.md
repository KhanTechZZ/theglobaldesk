Cambodia and China opened their eighth Golden Dragon joint military exercise on November 25. Cambodian military spokesperson Thong Solimo said this year's drills are the largest since the exercise began in 2016.

The 15-day exercise is scheduled to run through December 9. Its stated focus includes counterterrorism, humanitarian assistance, anti-piracy operations and disaster relief.

Cambodian Defence Minister Tea Seiha said the drills are not intended to threaten or provoke another country. The exercise comes amid wider attention to China and Cambodia's growing military ties and the expansion of Cambodia's Ream Naval Base. Cambodia has denied granting China special privileges or establishing a foreign military base there.
